# SYNTHETIC Employee and Contractor Offboarding Policy

Synthetic demonstration evidence only. No real employee information is present.

Effective: 2026-07-10

Employee and contractor offboarding requires the manager to notify Human Resources and IT when a departure is confirmed.

Access, accounts, and credentials must be removed within 24 hours of the recorded departure time.

IT must record the removal timestamp and ticket identifier so the completed offboarding action can be verified.
