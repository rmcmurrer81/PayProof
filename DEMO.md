# PayProof Atlas demonstration

Use the [86-second timed script](docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md) for the final recording. It is designed to stay within the required 60–90 second window.

## Elevator pitch

PayProof Atlas is a bookkeeping and risk assistant for small businesses. It brings bank activity, read-only email evidence, receipts, employee intake, and security records into a readable live dashboard. Deterministic rules calculate the money and match records; the assistant explains the result in plain language, cites the source, marks conflicts or missing facts, and never moves money.

## What to show

1. Start in **Meridian Works** and point to the clickable status cards, 3D evidence atlas, company picker, and truthful last/next refresh label.
2. Open the backup control to show a cited conflict and a targeted follow-up instead of a guessed answer.
3. Switch to **Personal Example** and ask: `What did the $43.57 Amazon bank transaction buy?`
4. Show that the bank row and email match on amount, currency, date, merchant, and order reference before the email's coffee filters and sparkling water are added.
5. Ask where spending could be cut, then open **People & spending** to show employee names, report totals, missing receipts, and items needing review.
6. Open **Sources & settings** to show that banks, read-only Gmail, and custom intake folders belong to the selected company. Point out the 5/10-minute refresh choice.
7. Mention reversible company archive and the credential-free, browser-readable transfer package, then close with the Golden Rule: **evidence may inform a decision; it never authorizes one.**

## Honest demo boundaries

- All bundled companies, people, transactions, emails, and documents are synthetic.
- Auto refresh rescans configured bank, Gmail, and assigned intake-folder sources for the active company. It reports partial failures and never changes a value merely to look live.
- Tavily public-web research is optional, manual, and server-side. Search words are sent to Tavily, so use only public company/vendor terms. Results remain unverified leads and never close a finding automatically.
- Hands-free input is opt-in. Spoken replies are available only when the recording browser exposes an eligible Natural, Neural, or Online voice; text remains the honest fallback.
- Plaid, Gmail, Tavily, the runtime model, and PRISM must stay labeled **Not configured**, **Local fallback**, or **Needs key** unless that exact live integration was verified.
- A changed destination or unusual expense is an anomaly to review, not proof of fraud.
- PayProof never initiates a payment or automatically approves an expense.

## Likely judge questions

**Why use AI?**

People ask financial and control questions in many ways. Deterministic Python performs the calculations and evidence matching; the optional model can only reword a bounded, validated evidence packet.

**Can it handle multiple companies?**

Yes. Branding, banks, Gmail authorization, intake folders, evidence, people, chat context, and refresh activity are scoped to the selected company.

**What does Tavily add?**

It provides an optional public-web lead when the company's own records do not answer a question. The saved page title, URL, excerpt, and retrieval time are labeled unverified until a person checks an authoritative source.

**What happens without provider keys or internet access?**

The synthetic demo, local statement and intake import, calculations, evidence views, deterministic assistant, company management, and review workflow continue locally. Unavailable services are shown as unavailable rather than reported as successful.

**Can a buyer read an exported company without PayProof?**

Yes. The transfer ZIP contains browser-readable HTML, CSV, JSON, a reading guide, and a manifest. Connector credentials and protected tokens are excluded; the buyer connects new bank and email authorization separately.
